# Template 04 · High-standards checklist (with a memo check)

Run on one domain at a time: a document type, a product flow, a process, a role. Source:
`references/06-high-standards.md`, `references/11-memos-not-slides.md` [L2017, LEX405, ECW18]. The four elements
are his; the checklist is ours.

> "Unrealistic beliefs on scope – often hidden and undiscussed – kill high standards." [L2017]

## Memo check (for six-page narratives)

- [ ] Problem-solving meeting, not a recurring metrics review (that one gets a dashboard and keeps to time
  [LEX405 02:05:07])
- [ ] Author had a week or more [L2017]
- [ ] Full sentences and paragraphs; no bullet-point arguments [LEX405 02:04:20]
- [ ] Shared with colleagues to improve, set aside, edited again [L2017]
- [ ] Read silently in the room before discussion [LEX405 02:00:51]
- [ ] No author names; the memo is the team's [L2017]
- [ ] Junior people speak first in the discussion (chapter 08)
