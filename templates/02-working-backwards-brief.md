# Template 02 · The working-backwards brief

Start from the customer and work back to the skills you need. Write it before any new product or major
feature. Sources: `references/02-customer-obsession-and-working-backwards.md`,
`references/03-what-wont-change-and-long-term-thinking.md`, `references/13-differentiation-and-creating-more-than-you-consume.md`
[L2008, L2009, L2006, ECW18]. The structure is ours. (A press release and FAQ format is often associated with
Amazon, but it isn't in this playbook's sources, so this brief doesn't claim to be it.)

> "Start with customers, and work backwards. Listen to customers, but don't just listen to customers – also
> invent on their behalf." [L2009]
