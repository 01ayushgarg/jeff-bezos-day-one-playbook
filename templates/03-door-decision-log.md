# Template 03 · The door decision log

Sort every open decision into one-way and two-way doors, push the two-way ones down and speed them up, and
slow the one-way ones down. Source: `references/05-one-way-and-two-way-doors.md` [L2015, L2016, LEX405].
Thresholds and columns are ours.

Rules (ours, from his):
- **Two-way doors:** one named person or a small team, deep in the organization [LEX405 01:01:29]. Deadline in
  days. Decide at about 70% of the information you wish you had [L2016].
- **One-way doors:** go to the most senior people, who slow them down and ask for more analysis
  [LEX405 01:00:06]. No deadline pressure from this log.
- **Every two-way door gets a check date.** Speed only works if you correct bad decisions quickly [L2016].
